## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a differentiated visual language for high-stakes enterprise deal flow, mergers & acquisitions, and operations management. It diverges from clinical, flat enterprise software by introducing soft tactile claymorphism balanced with surgical minimalism. 

The emotional signature is calm confidence, spatial order, and approachable authority. Enterprise decision-makers interact with substantial, matte-finished surfaces that feel physically sculpted rather than digitally stamped. Visual density is managed through generous negative space and disciplined color placement, preventing the dimensional treatment from overwhelming complex analytical workflows.

Key aesthetic characteristics:
- **Matte Volume:** Soft volumetric curvature created through dual-source ambient lighting, directional key shadows, and micro-inner bevel highlights.
- **Physical Extrusion vs. Carving:** Interactive elements protrude outward in resting states and compress inward when engaged; fields, wells, and troughs appear debossed directly into surfaces.
- **Organic Harmony:** A soothing botanical foundation of sage, warm porcelain, and sun-bleached cream, punctuated by muted status indicators that avoid digital harshness.

## Colors

The palette grounds high-pressure transaction management in natural, earthy tones. All colors are calibrated for matte light diffusion without spectral glare.

### Primary & Accent Roles
- **Primary Dark Sage (`#A8B5A0`):** Core structural accent, used for primary action backgrounds, toggle active tracks, focus rings, and primary interactive indicators.
- **Light Sage / Accent (`#C7D3C1`):** Secondary structural accent, pill borders, subtle fills, hover states, and light clay extrusions.
- **Tertiary Ochre (`#E6BA7E`):** Pending approvals, term sheet warnings, valuation holds, and secondary calls-to-attention.

### Canvas & Surface Matrix
- **App Canvas Base (`#F2F5EF`):** Global canvas background. Low-saturation cream-tinted sage that serves as the base plane from which elements extrude or carve.
- **Pure Cream Surfaces (`#FAFAF7`):** Elevated cards, contextual drawers, popovers, and floating toolbars. Acts as the primary elevated surface tier.
- **Debossed Surface Troughs (`#EBEEE7`):** Recessed canvas color for form inputs, table header troughs, and filter tracks.

### Typography & Structure
- **Text Ink Deep (`#3D483A`):** High-contrast headline and quantitative figures; deep olive slate with zero harsh neutral black.
- **Text Ink Muted (`#5A6956`):** Secondary descriptions, table body content, and inactive tab labels.
- **Text Ink Subdued (`#889982`):** Placeholder text, inactive column headers, metadata stamps, and dividing lines.

### Functional Status Indicators
- **Soft Emerald (`#8EBA97`):** Completed stages, executed agreements, favorable EBITDA trends.
- **Soft Coral (`#E58B88`):** Blockers, rejected offers, security flags, churn risks.
- **Soft Ochre (`#E6BA7E`):** In-review milestones, conditional offers, escrow holds.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes geometric clarity with approachable, humane warmth through **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its rounded terminals and open apertures harmonize with the soft clay geometry while preserving strict horizontal baseline metrics for dense financial reporting.

For numerical deal matrices, internal rates of return (IRR), cap tables, and balance sheets, **JetBrains Mono** is introduced strictly as a tabular utility face (`mono-data`).

Rules for enterprise typography application:
- Deal valuations, metrics, and currency units above 20px must enable tabular numerals (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1`).
- Headings maintain tight letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to bind multi-word transactional phrases into clear optical anchors.
- All micro-labels, badge texts, and status chips apply bold or semi-bold weights with tracked letter spacing to maintain legibility against rounded pill contours.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a balanced 12-column fluid responsive grid anchored by fixed navigational rail architectures. Because claymorphic components have volumetric footprint requirements (accounting for diffuse drop shadows), spacing increments follow an 8pt base grid with 4pt micro-steps.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column fluid grid, 24px gutters, fixed 280px left navigation rail, dynamic workspace margin (minimum 32px). Maximum bounded content container width is 1536px (`96rem`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 20px gutters, collapsed 72px iconized navigational rail, 24px outer margins. Multi-column financial widgets reflow to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout, 16px gutters, 16px outer margins. Navigation collapses to a bottom clay dock. Deal pipeline stages collapse from horizontal kanban boards into vertically stacked accordion swimlanes.

### Component Spacing Rhythm
Clay elements require dedicated shadow bleed zones. Surfaces must not sit flush against each other; maintain a minimum of `space-md` (16px) between standalone clay containers to allow diffuse ambient shadows to dissipate without visual mudding.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is modeled as physical clay extrusions from the canvas plane (`#F2F5EF`). Lighting is consistent across the interface: a primary light source positioned at top-left (`-45deg`), casting both a bright specular highlight along the upper-left edge and a soft, sage-tinted shadow along the bottom-right perimeter.

### Elevation Levels

#### Level 0: Debossed / Carved Surfaces (Troughs & Wells)
Used for text fields, search bars, empty drop zones, and switch track beds. The element is physically pressed below the canvas.
- **Formula:** `box-shadow: inset 3px 3px 6px rgba(168, 181, 160, 0.22), inset -2px -2px 5px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85);`
- **Surface Fill:** `#EBEEE7`

#### Level 1: Flat In-Canvas Surfaces
Resting panels, inactive table rows, neutral badges.
- **Formula:** No outer drop shadow; defined by a soft tonal transition and a 1px border (`rgba(168, 181, 160, 0.35)`).

#### Level 2: Low Extrusion (Clay Resting Elements)
Standard cards, transaction summary tiles, and unselected filter chips.
- **Formula:** `box-shadow: 6px 6px 14px rgba(168, 181, 160, 0.20), -5px -5px 12px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.90), inset 1px 1px 2px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.70);`
- **Surface Fill:** `#FAFAF7`

#### Level 3: Medium Extrusion (Interactive & Hero Elements)
Primary buttons, active deal cards, floating summary headers.
- **Formula:** `box-shadow: 10px 10px 20px rgba(168, 181, 160, 0.28), -7px -7px 16px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95), inset 1.5px 1.5px 3px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80);`
- **Surface Fill:** Dependent on component (`#A8B5A0` for primary actions; `#FAFAF7` for elevated modules).

#### Level 4: High Extrusion (Overlays & Modals)
Drawer panels, transaction sign-off sheets, quick-look previews.
- **Formula:** `box-shadow: 18px 18px 36px rgba(168, 181, 160, 0.32), -12px -12px 28px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95), inset 1px 1px 2px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.60);`

#### Level -1: Pressed / Active State (Physical Depress)
Triggers when a Level 2 or Level 3 button/chip is clicked or toggled active.
- **Formula:** `box-shadow: inset 3px 3px 6px rgba(61, 72, 58, 0.20), inset -2px -2px 4px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.50);`
- **Transform:** `transform: scale(0.985);`

## Shapes

The shape system employs generous, inflated corner radii that reinforce the clay visual metaphor. Elements avoid sharp 90-degree corners; sharp vertices destroy the optical illusion of molded clay.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Pill Shape (`9999px`):** Primary action buttons, filter tags, status badges, avatar badges, and search inputs.
- **Large Sculpted Radius (`24px` / `rounded-2xl`):** Standard data cards, module headers, metric containers, table wrappers.
- **Extra-Large Sculpted Radius (`32px` / `rounded-3xl`):** Deal pipeline stage columns, global side navigation, analytical modal sheets.
- **Micro Radius (`12px` / `rounded-lg`):** Nested child items, data table action icon buttons, tooltip surfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Clay Button:**
  - Shape: Pill (`border-radius: 9999px`).
  - Fill: `#A8B5A0`.
  - Typography: `label-lg`, Color: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Shadow: Level 3 extrusion (`10px 10px 20px rgba(168, 181, 160, 0.28), -7px -7px 16px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95), inset 1.5px 1.5px 3px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.80)`).
  - Hover: Subtle vertical lift (`transform: translateY(-1px)`).
  - Active / Pressed: Level -1 inset shadow with `transform: scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary Surface Button:**
  - Fill: `#FAFAF7`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(168, 181, 160, 0.3)`.
  - Typography: `label-lg`, Color: `#3D483A`.
  - Shadow: Level 2 extrusion.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges (Pills):**
  - Padding: `4px 12px`.
  - Border-radius: `9999px`.
  - **Success / Closed:** Fill `#E9F3EC`, Text `#3D6847`, Border `1px solid #C5E2CB`.
  - **Pending / Review:** Fill `#FBF3E6`, Text `#785624`, Border `1px solid #F1DAB6`.
  - **Danger / Halted:** Fill `#FAEDED`, Text `#803835`, Border `1px solid #F0C4C2`.
- **Interactive Filter Chips:**
  - Inactive: Level 2 extrusion, `#FAFAF7` background, text `#5A6956`.
  - Active: Debossed recessed state, fill `#EBEEE7`, text `#3D483A`, inset clay shadow.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Numerical Inputs:**
  - Height: `44px`.
  - Shape: Pill (`9999px`) or Rounded-xl (`16px`).
  - Fill: `#EBEEE7` (Debossed into surface).
  - Shadow: Level 0 debossed shadow (`inset 3px 3px 6px rgba(168, 181, 160, 0.22), inset -2px -2px 5px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`).
  - Border: None in resting state. On focus: `1.5px solid #A8B5A0`.
  - Text: `body-md` in `#3D483A`; Placeholder in `#889982`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Size: `22px x 22px`.
  - Unchecked: Level 0 debossed well, rounded-md (`6px` for checkbox, full round for radio).
  - Checked: Animated fill to `#A8B5A0` with an extruded white clay pip or checkmark (`inset 1px 1px 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.8)`).

### Cards & Pipeline Surfaces
- **Deal Summary Cards:**
  - Background: `#FAFAF7`.
  - Border-radius: `24px`.
  - Padding: `24px`.
  - Shadow: Level 2 extrusion.
  - Interaction: Draggable pipeline cards lift to Level 3 extrusion on hover/drag.
- **Debossed Data Tables:**
  - The outer table container uses `#FAFAF7` with Level 2 extrusion.
  - Table headers sit in an inset debossed track (`#EBEEE7`).
  - Table rows have no dividing borders; rows use alternating whitespace with rounded hover backgrounds (`#F2F5EF`). Numerical figures use `mono-data`.

### Enterprise Deal Flow Components
- **Stage Progress Track (Swimlane Pipeline):**
  - Continuous recessed trough (`#EBEEE7`) spanning the horizontal viewport.
  - Completed deal stages render as pill segments extruded in Light Sage (`#C7D3C1`) with connected inner conduits.
- **Valuation Metric Blocks:**
  - High-impact KPI blocks showing EV/EBITDA multiples and total deal size.
  - Carved metric wells containing high-contrast deep olive numerals with mono-spaced cents/decimals.
